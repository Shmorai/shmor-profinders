What can a business consultant help with?

  • Growth strategy and business planning
  • Business plans for funding and investment
  • Operations and process improvement
  • Pricing, sales and go-to-market strategy
  • Turnaround and cost reduction
  • Mentoring for founders and leadership teams

When should you hire a business consultant?

  • Growth has stalled and you are not sure why
  • You are raising finance and need a credible plan
  • You are scaling and processes are breaking
  • You want an experienced sounding board

Questions to ask before you hire

When you compare quotes, these questions help you find the right fit:

  1. What measurable results have you delivered for clients?
  2. Do you have experience in my industry?
  3. How do you structure engagements and fees?
  4. What will you need from my team?

What affects the cost?

Every business and project is different, so prices vary. The main factors are:

  • Length and scope of the engagement
  • Consultant’s seniority and specialism
  • Day-rate vs. project fee vs. retainer
  • Workshops, research and deliverables included

The simplest way to understand the cost for your situation is to compare a few quotes side by side.

Business Consultants: frequently asked questions

What does a business consultant actually do?
They diagnose problems and opportunities in your business and help you build and implement a plan — covering strategy, operations, sales, finance or people.
Is a business consultant worth it for a small business?
It can be, if the engagement is focused on a clear outcome. Agree goals and deliverables up front so you can measure the return.
How is a consultant different from a coach or mentor?
Consultants usually diagnose and recommend specific solutions; coaches and mentors help you develop your own thinking and skills. Some professionals offer both.
