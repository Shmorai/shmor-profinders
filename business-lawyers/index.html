What can a business lawyer help with?

  • Commercial contracts and supplier agreements
  • Terms and conditions and website terms
  • Shareholder and partnership agreements
  • Company formation and restructuring
  • Buying or selling a business
  • Commercial disputes and debt recovery

When should you hire a business lawyer?

  • You are signing a significant contract or partnership deal
  • You are bringing in investors or new shareholders
  • You need robust terms of business or website terms
  • A customer or supplier dispute is escalating

Questions to ask before you hire

When you compare quotes, these questions help you find the right fit:

  1. Have you handled similar matters for businesses in my sector?
  2. Can you give a fixed quote for this piece of work?
  3. What do you need from me to get started?
  4. What is your typical turnaround time?

What affects the cost?

Every business and project is different, so prices vary. The main factors are:

  • Type and complexity of the document or transaction
  • Whether you need drafting, review or negotiation
  • Urgency and turnaround time
  • Fixed-fee vs. hourly arrangements

The simplest way to understand the cost for your situation is to compare a few quotes side by side.

Business & Commercial Lawyers: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a commercial lawyer and a corporate lawyer?
Commercial lawyers typically focus on day-to-day trading contracts and relationships, while corporate lawyers focus on company structure, investment, mergers and acquisitions. Many firms do both.
Do I really need a lawyer for my contracts?
For low-value, routine work, templates may be enough. For anything significant, a lawyer can spot risks you might miss and make sure the contract actually protects you.
Can I get a fixed fee?
Many business lawyers offer fixed fees for defined pieces of work such as shareholder agreements or terms and conditions. Ask for this when you compare quotes.
