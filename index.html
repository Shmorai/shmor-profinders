  • People and legal: HR consultants, employment lawyers and business and commercial lawyers.
  • Finance: accountants and bookkeepers.
  • Marketing and digital: social media marketing and web designers.
  • Operations and growth: business consultants and IT support.

Not sure who you need?

  • Staff issue or disciplinary? Start with an HR consultant. If a claim is likely, speak to an employment lawyer.
  • Tax, VAT or year-end accounts? You need an accountant. For day-to-day records, a bookkeeper.
  • Need more customers? Look at social media marketing and web design.

How to choose a professional you can trust

  • Check credentials. Lawyers should be licensed by a state bar (US) or regulated by the SRA or their national law society (UK/EU). Accountants are often CPAs (US) or members of ICAEW, ACCA or CIMA (UK).
  • Read independent reviews and ask for references from similar businesses.
  • Agree scope and price in writing before work starts.
  • Compare at least two or three quotes.
