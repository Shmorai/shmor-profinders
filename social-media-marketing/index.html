What can a social media manager help with?

  • Social media strategy and content calendars
  • Content creation — posts, reels and short video
  • Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and LinkedIn management
  • Paid social advertising campaigns
  • Community management and engagement
  • Reporting and performance analysis

When should you hire a social media manager?

  • You know you should be posting but never find the time
  • Your posts get little engagement or no enquiries
  • You want to run paid ads without wasting budget
  • You are launching a new product, service or brand

Questions to ask before you hire

When you compare quotes, these questions help you find the right fit:

  1. Can you show results from clients similar to us?
  2. Which platforms do you specialise in?
  3. Who creates the content, and how is it approved?
  4. How will you report on results each month?

What affects the cost?

Every business and project is different, so prices vary. The main factors are:

  • Number of platforms and posts per month
  • Whether video or photography is included
  • Paid ad management and ad spend
  • Freelancer vs. agency

The simplest way to understand the cost for your situation is to compare a few quotes side by side.

Social Media Marketing Experts: frequently asked questions

Should I hire a freelancer or an agency?
Freelancers are often more flexible and cost-effective for smaller businesses. Agencies offer broader teams and capacity. Comparing quotes from both helps you decide.
How long until social media shows results?
Organic growth typically builds over several months. Paid campaigns can drive traffic much faster. A good specialist will set realistic expectations up front.
Which platform is best for my business?
It depends on where your customers spend time. B2B businesses often focus on LinkedIn, while consumer brands may prioritise Instagram, TikTok or Facebook.
