What can an accountant help with?

  • Year-end accounts and business tax returns
  • Personal tax returns for sole traders, freelancers and owners
  • VAT or sales tax registration and returns
  • Payroll and workplace pensions
  • Management accounts and cash-flow forecasting
  • Tax planning and business advice

When should you hire an accountant?

  • You are setting up a company (LLC, corporation or limited company) or going self-employed
  • Tax deadlines and paperwork (IRS, HMRC or your local tax office) are taking too much of your time
  • Your business is growing and you need better financial visibility
  • You want proactive tax planning, not just compliance

Questions to ask before you hire

When you compare quotes, these questions help you find the right fit:

  1. Are you a CPA (US) or a member of a recognised accountancy body such as ICAEW, ACCA or CIMA (UK)?
  2. Do you specialise in businesses of my size or sector?
  3. Which accounting software do you work with?
  4. Is your fee fixed monthly or charged per job?

What affects the cost?

Every business and project is different, so prices vary. The main factors are:

  • Business structure (sole trader, partnership, LLC or limited company)
  • Turnover and number of transactions
  • Services needed (VAT, payroll, advisory)
  • Quality of your existing records

The simplest way to understand the cost for your situation is to compare a few quotes side by side.

Accountants: frequently asked questions

Do I need an accountant as a small business?
It is not usually a legal requirement, but most incorporated businesses use one. An accountant helps you meet deadlines, avoid penalties and plan your tax efficiently.
What is the difference between an accountant and a bookkeeper?
Bookkeepers record day-to-day transactions. Accountants prepare statutory accounts and tax returns, and give financial and tax advice. Many businesses use both.
Can I switch accountants mid-year?
Yes. Your new accountant will usually contact your previous one for the information they need, so switching is straightforward.
