What can an IT support provider help with?

  • Helpdesk and remote support
  • Cyber security and data protection
  • Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace setup
  • Backups and disaster recovery
  • Networks, Wi-Fi and hardware
  • Cloud migration and IT strategy

When should you hire an IT support provider?

  • You rely on a staff member “who is good with computers”
  • You have had a security scare or data loss
  • You are moving to the cloud or opening a new office
  • You need predictable monthly IT costs

Questions to ask before you hire

When you compare quotes, these questions help you find the right fit:

  1. What are your guaranteed response times?
  2. Do you provide on-site support as well as remote?
  3. How do you handle cyber security and backups?
  4. Is support charged per user, per device or per hour?

What affects the cost?

Every business and project is different, so prices vary. The main factors are:

  • Number of users and devices
  • Response-time guarantees (SLA)
  • Security and backup services included
  • Pay-as-you-go vs. managed monthly contract

The simplest way to understand the cost for your situation is to compare a few quotes side by side.

IT Support Providers: frequently asked questions

What is a managed IT service?
A managed service provider looks after your IT proactively for a fixed monthly fee — monitoring, patching, security, backups and helpdesk support.
Do small businesses need cyber security support?
Yes. Small businesses are frequent targets of phishing and ransomware. Basic protections and backups are essential, and an IT provider can put them in place.
Can IT support be fully remote?
Most issues can be fixed remotely. Many providers combine remote support with on-site visits when needed.
