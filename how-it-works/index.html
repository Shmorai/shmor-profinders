How ProFinders works

Hiring a professional shouldn’t mean hours of searching and phone calls. Here’s how ProFinders gets you from problem to quotes.

Photo: Cytonn Photography on Unsplash

1. Tell us what you need

Choose a service and answer a few quick questions about your business and project.

2. Get matched

We connect your request with partner platforms of independent professionals.

3. Compare and choose

Review responses, compare quotes and experience, and hire only if you’re happy.

What happens after I request quotes?

When you choose to get quotes, you’re taken to one of our partner platforms to describe your project. Independent professionals who match your request can then contact you or send a quote.

Do I have to hire anyone?

No. Requesting quotes is free and there’s no obligation. Compare the responses and only go ahead if you find the right fit.

How we make money

ProFinders is part of the Shmor.ai network. Some links are referral links, so a partner platform may pay us a fee when we introduce you. This never changes what you pay. More about how we make money.

Tips for choosing a professional

  • Check qualifications and, for lawyers and accountants, licensing or regulatory status.
  • Read independent reviews and ask for references.
  • Get the scope and price agreed in writing.
  • Compare at least two or three quotes.
