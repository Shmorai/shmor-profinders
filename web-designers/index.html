What can a web designer help with?

  • New business websites and redesigns
  • E-commerce stores (Shopify, WooCommerce)
  • WordPress and Webflow sites
  • Landing pages for campaigns
  • Search-engine-friendly site structure
  • Ongoing maintenance and hosting

When should you hire a web designer?

  • You do not have a website, or it looks out of date
  • Your site does not work well on mobile
  • You want to sell products online
  • Your site gets visitors but few enquiries

Questions to ask before you hire

When you compare quotes, these questions help you find the right fit:

  1. Can I see live examples of sites you have built?
  2. Will I be able to update the content myself?
  3. Who owns the site and domain once it is finished?
  4. Is SEO and ongoing support included?

What affects the cost?

Every business and project is different, so prices vary. The main factors are:

  • Number of pages and features
  • Template-based vs. fully custom design
  • E-commerce and integrations
  • Copywriting, photography and SEO

The simplest way to understand the cost for your situation is to compare a few quotes side by side.

Web Designers: frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a website?
A simple business website can take a few weeks; larger or e-commerce sites take longer. Your designer should give you a clear timeline in their quote.
Do I need a custom website or a template?
Templates can be quick and cost-effective. Custom designs suit businesses that need specific features or a distinctive brand.
Will my website be mobile friendly?
It should be. Any professional web designer today builds responsive sites — confirm it when comparing quotes.
